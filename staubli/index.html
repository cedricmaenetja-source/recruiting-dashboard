Metric definitions & calculations
How each metric is calculated
MetricDefinitionCalculation (as computed here)
Time to HireAverage days from candidate application to hire.Mean of (Hired Date − New Date) across hired applications.
Time to FillAverage days from job posting to hire.Mean of (Hired Date − Job Ad Creation Date) across hired applications.
Number of ApplicationsCount of applications received.Total application rows, grouped by Job ID.
App → InterviewShare of applications that reached the interview stage.Applications with an Interview Date ÷ all applications.
Interview → HireShare of interviewed applicants who were hired.Applications with both an Interview Date and a Hired Date ÷ applications with an Interview Date.
Interviews per HireNumber of interview rounds a hire went through.Mean, across hires, of how many interview sub-stage columns are populated — Video, On-Site, On-Site 2, Final. Presence counts as one round each (day values are ignored); the parent “Time In Application State: Interview” is excluded.
Offer Acceptance RateShare of extended offers that were accepted.Applications with both an Offer Date and a Hired Date ÷ applications with an Offer Date.
Source of HireChannel that produced each hire.Hired applications grouped by the selected dimension — Source, Sub source, or Source type (toggle).
Stage ConversionStep-by-step progression through the pipeline.Stages counted from the stage date columns (New → Submitted to manager → Interview → Offer → Hired). Each step = applications reaching both that stage and the next ÷ applications reaching that stage.
Positions Filled vs OpenRecruitment status of positions.Position rows counted Filled/Hired vs Open (DELETED excluded), by the selected dimension (geography / unit / division / recruiter). Positions file merged on Job ID.
Job StatusRequisition pipeline: jobs and their applications per status.Stacked bar per status — Jobs counted by distinct Job ID (from the Jobs export when loaded, so Created & no-applicant jobs are included) and Applications counted from application rows. Order: Created → Sourcing → Interview → Offer → Filled.
Average Time in StageMean days spent in each pipeline state, shown as a heat map.Mean days in Lead, New, In-Review, Interview and Offered, broken down by Stäubli unit.
Average Time in Job StatusMean days a job spends in each job status, shown as a heat map (Cancelled excluded).Mean of the “Time In Job Status” columns (Created, Sourcing, Interview, Offer, On Hold) by Stäubli unit.
All metrics are “in period” — they respect the Job Creation Date range and every filter selected below.
